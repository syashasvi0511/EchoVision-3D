The blue wedge on the sonar display is your facing direction — obstacles ahead of you sound in front, obstacles to your side pan to that ear.
Sonification scheme: pan = horizontal angle · volume + pulse rate = distance · pitch = height (low tone = ground-level obstacle, high tone = head-height). Silence means the path ahead is clear. Rendered with the Web Audio API's HRTF panning model for true directional cues over headphones.
